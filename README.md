# 🎯 HeistPilot — The Ultimate PAYDAY 2 Player Companion Toolkit

> **HeistPilot** is an independent, community-driven enhancement suite for PAYDAY 2 that puts customization, quality-of-life tools, and gameplay insights directly into your hands — all wrapped in a sleek, responsive, and multilingual interface. Think of it as your co-pilot for every heist, from First World Bank to San Martín.

## 🧭 Overview

HeistPilot began as a small passion project shared among friends who loved PAYDAY 2 but wished for a more personal, flexible way to shape their experience. Over time, it grew into a fully-fledged companion platform offering a wide range of in-game utilities, interface tweaks, and performance enhancements — all without ever touching the core game files in ways that could compromise your save or your integrity.

Where other tools feel clunky, HeistPilot feels like a well-oiled drill: precise, fast, and satisfying to use. Every menu, every toggle, every setting has been tuned to reduce friction and let you focus on what matters — the heist itself.

## ✨ Feature Highlights

### 🎨 Interface & Experience
- **Responsive UI** — Adjusts gracefully across resolutions, from ultrawide monitors to modest laptops.
- **Dark & Light Themes** — Choose the visual mood that matches your gaming setup.
- **Multilingual Support** — Localization packs covering English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, and more, with community-contributed translations.
- **Custom Keybind Manager** — Map every action to the key of your choice, with conflict detection.

- **Loadout Presets** — Save and instantly swap between your favorite weapon and perk configurations.

### 🌐 Connectivity & Community
- **24/7 Customer Support** — A dedicated support team that responds to tickets day or night, across timezones.
- **Auto-Update Channel** — Stay on the latest stable release without manually checking.
- **Community Presets Hub** — Share and import loadouts or UI configurations with other players.

### 🚀 Performance & Reliability
- **Low Memory Footprint** — Runs alongside PAYDAY 2 without noticeable overhead.
- **Modular Architecture** — Enable or disable individual modules to suit your playstyle.
- **Crash-Resilient Sessions** — Auto-saves your current module state so a crash never costs you your setup.

## 🛠️ What Makes HeistPilot Different?

Most trainers and companion apps treat the player as a number. HeistPilot treats you as the driver. The metaphor here is simple: you're not just along for the ride in a getaway van — you're the wheelman, deciding the route, the speed, and the destination.

A few design principles we follow:

1. **Transparency First** — Every module explains what it does in plain language. No hidden toggles, no mysterious background processes.
2. **Respect for the Game** — HeistPilot is designed for personal, single-player or private-session enjoyment.
3. **Community Voices** — Feature requests from the community are reviewed weekly and often shipped within the same development cycle.
4. **Accessibility Awareness** — Multilingual support and colorblind-friendly palettes are treated as first-class features, not afterthoughts.
